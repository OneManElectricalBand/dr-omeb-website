A Practical Guide to Incorporating AI and ChatGPT in Everyday Life

As a marketing professor at Thomas More University, I’ve had the pleasure of exploring the incredible potential of AI, both in my personal life and in the classroom. During my recent talk at the Rotary Club of Florence, I discussed how artificial intelligence, specifically ChatGPT, can enhance productivity, streamline daily tasks, and support creativity. This blog post is a recap of that talk, focusing on practical ways you can use AI in your daily life.

Why AI Matters in Everyday Life

AI is already all around us—from personal assistants like Alexa and Siri to apps that help manage your finances or health. It’s about simplifying routine tasks, making smarter decisions, and ultimately freeing up your time for more important things.

So, how can you incorporate AI into your daily life? Here’s a quick rundown of what I shared:

  1. AI for Personal Life: Automate scheduling, manage your to-do lists, and keep track of finances with AI-powered tools.
  2. AI for Professional Life: Tools like ChatGPT can assist with drafting emails, writing reports, or even brainstorming creative ideas.
  3. AI in Education: AI can help both teachers and students by simplifying research, generating feedback, and even automating grading.

But there’s one tool that stands out for its versatility—ChatGPT. Whether you're a business owner, teacher, or student, ChatGPT can transform how you work, create, and solve problems.

The PARTS Method for Writing Effective Prompts

Writing effective prompts is the key to getting the most out of ChatGPT. This is where the PARTS method comes in. PARTS stands for:

  • Persona: Identify your role.
  • Aim: State your objective.
  • Recipients: Specify the audience.
  • Theme: Describe the style, tone, and related parameters.
  • Structure: Note the desired format of the output.

Let’s break it down with an example.

Imagine you’re a teacher wanting to create a case study on digital marketing for your students. Your prompt might look like this:

  • Persona: I am a Marketing Professor at a university.
  • Aim: Create a case study on digital marketing strategies.
  • Recipients: My students are undergraduate marketing students in their junior year.
  • Theme: Use an engaging and educational tone. Cite real-world examples.
  • Structure: Provide 5 options for case study topics in bullet points.

This simple framework ensures that your prompts are clear, focused, and deliver the most relevant results. Below are additional examples of prompts you can use in your everyday life.

ChatGPT Prompts You Can Use in Your Daily Life

Whether you're looking for professional or personal assistance, ChatGPT is a game-changer. Here are some prompts (following the PARTS method) you can start using today:

1. Persona: Busy professional

  • Aim: Draft an email requesting a meeting with a potential client.
  • Recipients: The client is a business executive at a local company.
  • Theme: Professional and polite, encouraging a positive response.
  • Structure: Email format, 3 short paragraphs.

Prompt for ChatGPT:
"I am a marketing consultant. Draft an email requesting a meeting with a business executive at a Graeter's Ice Cream. I want to talk about how I can help Graeter's use AI to improve their workflow. Keep the tone professional and polite, and encourage a positive response. Do not use complex sentence structures. The email should be 3 short paragraphs."

2. Persona: Parent planning a family vacation

  • Aim: Create an itinerary for a 5-day family vacation in a national park.
  • Recipients: My family includes two adults and two children, ages 8 and 12.
  • Theme: Fun and educational, balancing outdoor activities with relaxation.
  • Structure: Day-by-day itinerary with suggestions for activities.

Prompt for ChatGPT:
"I am a parent planning a family vacation to Colorado. Create a 5-day itinerary for my family of two adults and two children (ages 8 and 12) for a national park visit. What parks should we visit? Include fun and educational activities with a balance of outdoor adventures and relaxation. Provide a day-by-day itinerary."

3. Persona: Small business owner

  • Aim: Generate 5 creative social media post ideas to promote a new product.
  • Recipients: My target audience is young professionals (ages 25-35) interested in tech gadgets.
  • Theme: Engaging and trendy, highlighting product features in a fun way.
  • Structure: 5 bullet point ideas.

Prompt for ChatGPT:
"I am a small business owner who specializes in selling accessories for cell phones. Generate 5 creative social media post ideas to promote a sale I'm having on the new Sony Bluetooth Neckband Speaker. My target audience is young professionals aged 25-35 who are exercise enthusiasts. The tone should be engaging and trendy, with a focus on highlighting the product's features."

4. Persona: Student preparing for an exam

  • Aim: Summarize the key concepts of a marketing textbook.
  • Recipients: I am a college student studying for my final exam.
  • Theme: Clear and concise, focusing on the most important concepts.
  • Structure: Summary in bullet points.

Prompt for ChatGPT:
"I am a college student studying for an exam. Summarize the key concepts in the attached textbook about marketing. Keep the summary clear and concise, focusing on the most important concepts. Provide the summary in bullet points."

5. Persona: Home cook

  • Aim: Suggest a dinner recipe using chicken, rice, and vegetables.
  • Recipients: I am cooking for a family of four.
  • Theme: Simple and healthy, with easy-to-follow instructions.
  • Structure: Step-by-step recipe format.

Prompt for ChatGPT:
"I am a home cook preparing dinner for a family of four. Suggest a simple and healthy recipe using chicken, rice, and vegetables. Provide the instructions in a step-by-step format."

Start Incorporating AI Into Your Life Today

ChatGPT, when used with the PARTS method, can simplify and enhance how you approach both personal and professional tasks. Whether you're sending emails, creating content, or planning your next vacation, AI can help you save time and work smarter.
