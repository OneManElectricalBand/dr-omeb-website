## Verification notes

- Breaking the Law was confirmed in Spotify's live artist discography and single page, with track `6pAKuLgsaYzE84AS4sq63m`, release date August 25, 2025, and OMEB Music rights notice. Apple and Amazon agree. Removed the stale coming-soon label.
- Sign of the Times links to the OMEB Music release on all three services. The alternate Spotify track `1JUxp6Sj6AEQvZbq3mA9Fa` and Apple album `1786761296` show SphereTrax; they are not used here.
- Long Train Runnin' links specifically to the Sefi Carmel Remix.
- Stargazer links to the complete three-mix release on each service. Current services label the mixes CMS Mix, Sefi Carmel Master, and Dave Collins Master. Older mix-specific Spotify URLs were not reused.
- Spotify public pages sometimes expose only metadata to web retrieval. The live browser verified Cincinnati Vampires' artist discography, Breaking the Law, and the complete Stargazer release. Other direct destinations were checked through primary public service pages and metadata.
- Verification establishes the public destination and release identity; it is not a test of signed-in subscription playback in every territory.
