The Music Behind the Service

I am Dr. OMEB®. OMEB stands for One Man Electrical Band®. I build every supporting instrument myself, then step forward live with the lead guitar, vocals, and personality.

This is where my records, videos, and collaborations live: Dr. OMEB releases, Cincinnati Vampires, and sessions with musicians connected to Black Sabbath, Dio, Styx, LILIAC, and beyond.

Every instrument. Every layer. I am the band.
