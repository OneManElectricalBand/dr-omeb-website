Meet the man behind the entire band

Dr. OMEB

Dr. OMEB is not backed by the One Man Electrical Band. Dr. OMEB is THE One Man Electrical Band. Since 2003, he has built full-scale rock performances by recording every supporting instrument himself and then bringing the lead guitar, vocals, personality, and chaos to the stage live.

His career includes performances alongside major touring acts, collaborations with Vinny Appice and Todd Sucherman, more than a million plays on a breakout release, and a worldwide online audience that now gathers under one roof: Rock n' Roll Church.

The local stages built the act. Rock n' Roll Church turned it into a global congregation.
