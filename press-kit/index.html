01 · Copy-ready biography

Official Biography

Dr. OMEB is THE One Man Electrical Band—the artist, the act, and the creator of Rock n' Roll Church. Since 2003, he has built full-scale rock performances by recording every supporting instrument himself, then performing the lead guitar and vocals live.

Cincinnati stages built the act. More than 1,000 performances sharpened a show designed to deliver the sound and energy of a complete rock band through one performer, one technical footprint, and one booking conversation.

Dr. OMEB has appeared as featured local support at Riverbend Music Center on nights headlined by KISS, Mötley Crüe, Journey, Styx, Ted Nugent, Megadeth, and other major touring artists. His recording work includes collaborations with Vinny Appice of Black Sabbath and Dio and Todd Sucherman of Styx.

In 2025, the stage expanded. Rock n' Roll Church became a weekly global livestream gathering, helping grow the Dr. OMEB audience to more than 84,000 followers and more than 10 million career video views.

“Where's the band?” I am the band.

Technical Rider

Stage Requirements

  • Minimum 10×10 ft stage space
  • 2× standard 20A circuits
  • Stage monitoring (1–2 monitors)
  • Setup: 60–90 minutes
  • Breakdown: 30–45 minutes

PA / Sound

  • House PA preferred (can supply)
  • Stereo house mix from interface
  • Guitar DI or mic'd amp
  • Vocal mic + stand
  • Monitor send for vocals + guitar

Hospitality

  • Parking for 1 vehicle / load-in
  • Water / beverages on stage
  • Meal or buyout for 4hr+ shows
  • Day-of contact for emergencies
